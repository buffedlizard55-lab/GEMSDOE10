Thesis

Both prize rounds score faults missing from the catalogue, with known-catalogue pixels masked from evaluation. Optimising catalogue reproduction is the wrong objective — our sibling 6GEMSDOE reached 0.17 blocked-CV catalogue DTI and scored 0.0286 on the leaderboard. GEMSDOE10 selects on hidden-fault simulation: whole fault systems are held out, the model trains on the rest from geophysics alone, and policies are ranked by masked distance-weighted Tversky on the held-out systems.

harness: system-holdout ×5107 channels71/71 tests

Key findings

  • Catalogue proximity memorises (DTI 0.012) → removed
  • Geophysics-only HGB: masked DTI 0.0895 (fold 0, topk02_binary)
  • Unsupervised lidar ridge alone: 0.0254
  • SGMC-gap traces: 0.0000 → excluded (documented negative)
