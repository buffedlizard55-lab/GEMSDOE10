# Limitations and access needs

1. **No private new-fault labels or guaranteed leaderboard uplift.** Spatial CV still scores held-out catalogue faults. Component holdouts and FAR10 subsets are different proxies. Hidden source/type/coverage is explicitly undisclosed by staff. Neither can certify geothermal resources.
2. **No DrivenData account session.** We can read public leaderboard/forum through the research fetch tool, but cannot verify authenticated upload hashes, remaining slots or eligibility. Never request credentials in chat; use the authorized account holder's normal platform workflow.
3. **Binary network access is uneven.** Dropbox, GDR, USGS and DrivenData binary requests fail TLS from this runtime; Actions logs/artifacts are unreadable here. Public GitHub API/git transport works, so external products are built on a GitHub-hosted runner and moved through immutable `ext/*` tags (session 3). Feature data were restored from an immutable team mirror and matched inherited hashes, not independently authenticated official bytes. H15 archives are now in hand (hashed) but unmodelled.
4. **CPU only, ~4 GB RAM.** Bounded HGB training works here. A GPU is not required for the implemented experiment. Raw 1 m DEM processing (measured session 5: 686 tiles / 157 GB cover 98.3 % of the footprint) must run as a sharded GitHub-runner job; U-Net comparisons need additional compute and their own frozen protocols; no assertion that GPU training is already complete.
5. **No artifact without a positive experiment AND a bound final fit.** H12 was measured and rejected; H13 and H19 failed the preregistered rule (development improved, confirmation regressed); H16 (session 2), H20 (session 3) and H25 (session 4) are the released, hash-bound candidates — H25 (166 features, `ridge15_binary`) beat the same-run H20 arm and the H20/thin10 incumbent on the development mean and the confirmation fold and is now the hub's recommended download (`reports/h25_blocked.json`, `reports/final_manifest_h25.json`); H24's H20+ridge15 (dev 0.23553 / conf 0.22525) was eligible but not released. No submission slot was spent by any session; no artifact has a leaderboard measurement.
5b. **Hidden truth density is bounded, not measured.** Session 5 inverted the seven user-reported group scores (`reports/lb_probe.json`): the new-fault truth is ~0.13–0.6 % of the scored area, 2–8× sparser than the catalogue proxy. Protocol v5 therefore decides on a density-matched simulation (held-out catalogue thinned to 1/2 and 1/4 of its systems, the rest masked as known). The bound relies on user-reported scores (account/file association unauthenticated) and on FP ≈ public share × emitted pixels; the simulation keeps the catalogue's own spatial clustering, which the new faults may not share.
5b-2. **Known-fault shadow emission is still unmeasured.** Local score blocks contain no training faults, so emission hugging KNOWN faults is never penalised locally, while the final in-sample fit makes such shadow ridges likely (our released fields put 28–46 % of their mass within 300 m of catalogue faults vs 18.5 % for the best scored group artifact). Needs an in-sample masked-known protocol (NEXT_STEPS P1).
5c. **The provided labels are the current public catalogue** (session-3 measurement): no public catalogue offers held-out "new" traces, so every local proxy scores catalogue generalisation only.
6. **Feature semantics/normalization issues.** `tc` and depth-to-base metadata need official release comparison. Existing Frangi normalization depends on tile-local maxima; new paired experiments fix the tile height and hash the resulting stack. Fixing this changes the baseline and needs a new recorded run.
7. **Masking surrogate is not backend source code.** Known pixels are excluded officially, but order relative to the metric dilation is not disclosed. The optional FP-ignore metric is an explicit surrogate, not a byte-for-byte official scorer claim.
8. **Current-best scope.** Reproducible persisted campaign arms are baseline/discovery/selftrain; some claimed external runs have no persisted grids/results. No claim of beating every historic group method or the neural ensemble that scored .1563 without matched retraining.
9. **Live checks are not continuous science.** Daily scheduled workflow checks availability, quotations and the leaderboard. Changed text or failures require review; it does not autonomously certify newly downloaded data, account compliance or geologic interpretations. GitHub schedules may be delayed/disabled by platform policy.
10. **Legal/user obligations remain.** Rules eligibility, AI-disclosure accuracy and account/entity submission allowance require participant attestation. A repo count is not proof of a rules violation. Final upload/sign-off cannot honestly be declared fully autonomous here.

Do not claim all original goals are finished: no demonstrated >.3049 model, no new geothermal-vent catalogue, no private-score verification; the 10 m DEM pilot (H20) is a first external-resolution test, not a 1 m lidar study. H16's +0.0037 dev / +0.0007 confirmation margin, H20's +0.0147 dev / +0.0141 confirmation margin and H25's +0.0011 dev / +0.0265 confirmation margin over the same-run H20 arm are catalogue-generalization proxies on four correlated stripe folds, not leaderboard forecasts; the confirmation fold is re-used geography (now for a fourth decision), the selected policies were chosen on the development folds from a 22-policy set, H25 loses fold 0 to same-run H20 and its development margin is within fold noise, and the emission budget was tuned to the catalogue's density rather than the unknown hidden density (`reports/budget_density_sweep.json`, thin family only). See HYPOTHESES.md (session-2 results) for the next measurable work.
